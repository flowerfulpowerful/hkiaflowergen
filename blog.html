Percentage Toggle Guide - Understanding Flower Chances

August 7, 2025 Tips

Understanding flower percentages is key to successful breeding! The Percentage Toggle feature lets you switch between standard and greenhouse percentages to see your exact chances of getting each flower.

How to Use the Percentage Toggle:

  • Look for the percentage (%) button in your generator interface
  • Click the button to switch between different percentage modes
  • The button's appearance will change to show which mode is active
  • Greenhouse percentages are automatically enabled when you first visit the site

Standard Percentages (Pink Ring):

  • Shows percentages for regular flower growing in any plot
  • These are the base chances for each flower type
  • Useful for understanding fundamental breeding mechanics
  • Good for learning how different plots affect flower chances

Greenhouse Percentages (Green Ring):

  • Shows percentages as if you're growing in the greenhouse
  • These percentages reflect the enhanced chances from greenhouse growing
  • More accurate for players who primarily use the greenhouse
  • Often shows higher chances for rare flowers

When to Use Each Mode:

  • Greenhouse Mode: Use when planning greenhouse gardens or if you mostly grow in the greenhouse
  • Standard Mode: Use when planning outdoor gardens or learning basic breeding mechanics
  • Comparison: Switch between modes to see how greenhouse affects your chances
  • Strategy Planning: Use both modes to plan the most efficient breeding strategy

Pro Tips:

  • Start with Greenhouse: Since it's the default, use greenhouse percentages for most planning
  • Compare Modes: Switch between modes to see which gives you better chances for specific flowers
  • Plan Your Strategy: Use the mode that matches your actual growing method
  • Learn the Differences: Understanding both modes helps you make informed breeding decisions
  • Optimize Your Garden: Use the mode that gives you the best chances for your target flowers

Understanding the Percentages:

  • Higher Percentages: Better chances of getting that flower
  • Lower Percentages: Rarer flowers that are harder to obtain
  • Zero Percentages: Flowers that aren't possible with your current setup
  • Mode Differences: Greenhouse often shows higher percentages for rare flowers

The Percentage Toggle helps you make informed decisions about your flower breeding strategy. Whether you're growing in the greenhouse or outdoors, understanding your chances is the key to creating the perfect garden!

Import & Export Guide - Share Your Flower Gardens!

August 7, 2025 Tips

Want to share your beautiful flower garden with friends or save your favorite layouts? The Import & Export feature lets you save and share your flower grids with just a few clicks!

How to Export Your Garden:

  • Create your perfect flower garden layout
  • Open the "Settings" menu
  • Click the "Export" button (look for the upward arrow icon)
  • Your grid code will automatically be copied to your clipboard
  • You can now paste this code anywhere to share with others

How to Import a Garden:

  • Open the "Settings" menu
  • Click the "Import" button (look for the downward arrow icon)
  • A text box will appear where you can paste a grid code
  • Paste the code you received from a friend or saved previously
  • Click the "Submit" button to load the garden
  • Your flower garden will be instantly recreated!

What Gets Saved:

  • Flower Types: All flower varieties and their exact positions
  • Colors: Every flower's color information
  • Effects & Patterns: Special effects and patterns on flowers
  • Plot Settings: Which plots are enabled or disabled
  • Garden Layout: Your complete garden design

Pro Tips:

  • Backup Your Favorites: Export your best gardens to save them permanently
  • Share with Friends: Send grid codes to friends so they can recreate your gardens
  • Community Sharing: Share your codes on forums or social media to help other players
  • Experiment Safely: Export before making major changes so you can always go back
  • Collect Rare Gardens: Import codes from other players to discover new flower combinations

Example Grid Code:

Grid codes look like this: H4sIAAAAAAAAA10qjlFKL8pMCc6sS...

These codes contain all the information needed to recreate your exact garden layout!

The Import & Export feature makes it easy to share your creativity with the Hello Kitty Island Adventure community. Save your favorite gardens, share them with friends, and discover amazing flower combinations from other players!

How to Use the Paint Tool - Complete Guide

August 7, 2025 Tips

The Paint Tool is one of the most powerful features in the HKIA Flower Generator! Use it to disable plots and copy/paste flowers to create your perfect garden layout.

How to Enable Paint Mode:

  • Look for the "Paint Mode" button in your generator interface
  • When the button is green, paint mode is enabled and ready to use
  • You can toggle paint mode on and off as needed

Disabling Plots:

  • With paint mode enabled, click on any empty plot
  • The plot will turn dark gray, indicating it's now disabled
  • Disabled plots won't generate flowers and won't be included in calculations
  • This is perfect for creating custom garden layouts or focusing on specific areas

Copy/Paste Flowers:

  • Click on a plot that contains an existing flower
  • Then click on an empty plot to copy the flower there
  • The flower will be duplicated with all its properties (type, color, effects, etc.)
  • This is great for creating symmetrical gardens or duplicating rare flowers

Pro Tips:

  • Plan Your Layout: Use paint mode to sketch out your garden design before generating
  • Create Patterns: Copy/paste flowers to create beautiful symmetrical patterns
  • Focus Breeding: Disable unwanted plots to concentrate on specific flower combinations
  • Experiment Freely: Paint mode lets you try different layouts without losing your work

The Paint Tool gives you complete control over your flower garden. Use it to create unique layouts, duplicate your favorite flowers, and design the perfect garden for your Hello Kitty Island Adventure!
